# Maya

## Who
Maya is a hobby photographer who edits on a tablet on the couch. She is patient with
new tools but gives up on anything that needs a manual.

## Why they're here
She wants to touch up last weekend's photos and share two of them tonight.

## Goals
- Open a photo from the keys in three presses or fewer.
  Counted: keys on open-photo, target 3, lower is better.
- The help is readable on a tablet.

## Script
1. Open my photo. Measured: open-photo
2. Crop it to a square.
3. Read the help page.

## Known
- Undo history is lost on reload.
